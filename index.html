Kitchen Remodeling Services for Every Style and Budget

From a targeted upgrade to a complete transformation, a professional remodeling team should offer the full range of services under one roof so that design, ordering and construction stay perfectly coordinated.

Full Kitchen Remodels

A full remodel reimagines the entire space. Walls may be opened, appliances relocated and finishes replaced from floor to ceiling. This approach delivers the biggest impact, giving you improved flow, better storage and a cohesive look where every element belongs.

Custom Cabinetry and Storage

Cabinets define both the look and the daily function of a kitchen. Custom and semi-custom options let you choose door styles, finishes, hardware and interior organizers such as pull-out trays, deep drawers, spice pullouts and tall pantry units that make every inch work harder.

Countertops and Backsplashes

Quartz, granite and natural stone remain popular for their beauty and durability. Paired with a carefully chosen backsplash, from classic subway tile to bold book-matched slabs, your surfaces become the visual centerpiece of the room.

Layout Changes and Islands

Improving the layout often delivers more value than any single finish. Adding an island, widening walkways or creating an open connection to the dining or living area turns a cramped kitchen into a natural gathering place.

Lighting, Flooring and Finishing Touches

Layered lighting with under-cabinet task lights, pendants and recessed fixtures brings warmth and depth. New flooring, fresh paint and thoughtful hardware finish the space and tie every design decision together.

Our Kitchen Remodeling Process, Step by Step

A clear process is the difference between a stressful renovation and an enjoyable one. Here is how a well-run kitchen remodeling project typically unfolds.

  1. Consultation and vision. We discuss your goals, lifestyle, wish list and budget, and walk through the existing space.
  2. Design and planning. Layouts, materials, finishes and appliances are selected, with drawings and specifications that remove guesswork.
  3. Written estimate and schedule. You receive a detailed scope, timeline and pricing so expectations are clear before work begins.
  4. Permits and ordering. Required permits are arranged and long-lead items such as cabinets and stone are ordered early.
  5. Demolition and construction. Old materials are removed, then framing, electrical, plumbing, drywall, cabinetry and countertops are installed in the proper sequence.
  6. Final details and walkthrough. Lighting, hardware and touch-ups are completed, followed by a walkthrough to confirm everything meets your standards.

Luxury Kitchen Design Trends Worth Considering

Trends come and go, but the strongest designs balance style with timeless appeal. These ideas are shaping premium kitchens right now.

  • Two-tone cabinetry: pairing deep tones with lighter uppers adds contrast and depth.
  • Statement islands: waterfall edges, contrasting stone and integrated seating create a true focal point.
  • Warm metals: brushed brass and champagne bronze hardware add a sophisticated glow.
  • Integrated appliances: panel-ready refrigerators and dishwashers keep lines clean and seamless.
  • Smart storage: hidden charging drawers, appliance garages and custom pantries reduce clutter.
  • Layered lighting: dimmable pendants, toe-kick lighting and glass-front display cabinets set the mood.

Choosing Materials That Look Stunning and Last

Premium does not have to mean fragile. Quartz resists stains and needs no sealing, while natural stone offers unmatched character with proper care. Solid wood or high-quality plywood cabinet boxes hold up to daily use far better than low-grade alternatives. For flooring, porcelain tile and engineered hardwood are both durable choices that suit busy households.

The same principle applies to hardware, faucets and fixtures. Well-made components operate smoothly for years, and they often cost less over time than cheaper replacements that wear out quickly.

Permits, Planning and Timelines

Many kitchen projects require permits, especially when they involve structural changes, electrical work, plumbing or gas lines. Requirements vary, so it is wise to confirm details with the City of Palos Hills building department and to work with a contractor who handles the paperwork properly.

Timelines depend on scope. Ordering custom cabinets can take several weeks, and construction itself follows a specific sequence. Setting up a temporary kitchen with a microwave, small refrigerator and a countertop burner keeps daily life comfortable while work is underway.

How to Choose the Right Kitchen Remodeling Contractor

A contractor who listens carefully, explains options honestly and documents everything in writing is far more likely to deliver a result you love.

Kitchen Remodeling FAQs

How long does a kitchen remodel take in Palos Hills?

A typical full remodel takes several weeks once construction begins, but custom cabinet lead times and permits can extend the overall schedule. You should receive a written timeline before work starts.

Do I need a permit for a kitchen remodel?

Projects involving structural, electrical, plumbing or gas changes usually do. Confirm requirements with the City of Palos Hills and your contractor.

Can I stay in my home during the remodel?

Yes, most homeowners do. A temporary kitchen setup makes it manageable, and a good contractor keeps the work area clean and contained.

What adds the most value to a kitchen?

Improved layout, quality cabinetry, durable countertops and good lighting consistently make the biggest difference in both daily enjoyment and long-term appeal.
